Matsui Laboratory

Bionanotechnology and nanotechnology at the Department of Chemistry, Hunter College, City University of New York.

About

Our group, led by Professor Hiroshi Matsui, works at the interface of nanotechnology and biotechnology. Our research spans bionanotechnology, biomaterials, sensors, electronics, medical imaging and quantum computing. Professor Matsui also teaches biophysical chemistry, physical chemistry and biological spectroscopy at Hunter.

The lab has developed a range of nanoparticles, including inorganic nanocages, and uses peptide frameworks to assemble nanomaterials in three dimensions.
