About

Lakshika Malwana is a Ph.D. student in Biochemistry at the City University of New York Graduate Center. She joined the Matsui Laboratory at Hunter College in Spring 2025. Her research interests include protein biochemistry, polymer chemistry, nanotechnology, and the development of biomaterials for biomedical and sustainable material applications. Her work explores interactions between biological molecules and engineered materials, with an emphasis on their properties and potential applications.

Research

Lakshika's research focuses on protein-material interactions and the application of nanotechnology to biological systems. Her work includes investigating interactions between protein surfaces and polymer surfactants to improve protein stability and facilitate the incorporation of proteins into biodegradable polymer materials. She uses analytical techniques including circular dichroism spectroscopy, FTIR, solid-state NMR, thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), and differential scanning calorimetry (DSC).

Her research also includes exploring the use of iron oxide nanocages to manipulate the extracellular matrix of cancer tissues, with the goal of understanding how changes in the matrix may influence cancer cell invasiveness.
