Principal Investigator

Hiroshi Matsui, Ph.D.

Professor of Chemistry, Hunter College, The City University of New York

Professor Hiroshi Matsui teaches courses in biophysical chemistry and physical chemistry in the Department of Chemistry at Hunter College. His research focuses on bionanotechnology, nanotechnology, biomaterials, sensors, electronics, medical imaging and quantum computing. He is also an Adjunct Professor at Weill Cornell Medicine and an affiliate faculty member of the Nanoscience Initiative at the CUNY Advanced Science Research Center.

Education

  • Postdoctoral research, Columbia University (1996–1998)
  • Ph.D., Chemistry, Purdue University (1996)
  • M.S., Materials Science and Engineering, Stanford University (1991)
  • B.S., Sophia University (1987)

Appointments

  • Professor, Hunter College (2008–present)
  • Adjunct Professor, Weill Cornell Medicine (2015–present)
  • Department Chair, Hunter College (2010–2013)
  • Associate Professor, Hunter College (2004–2007)
  • Assistant Professor, Hunter College (2001–2003)

Courses taught

  • CHM 35000 Biophysical Chemistry
  • CHM 35600 Physical Chemistry
  • CHM 37100 Biological Spectroscopy
